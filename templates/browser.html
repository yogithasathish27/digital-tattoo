🌐 Browser Privacy Scan

Digital Tattoo can also show you what your own web browser reveals to the websites you visit. This educational scan runs entirely inside your browser using standard browser APIs and is never a virus, malware check or security breach test.

🧬 What does your browser reveal?

When you click the button below, this page reads only the information a normal website is allowed to read from your browser, such as your browser name, screen size, language, time zone and hardware hints. It also reports the current permission status for location, camera, microphone and notifications.

Digital Tattoo does not switch on your camera or microphone, does not request your GPS coordinates, does not save a persistent fingerprint, and does not send any of this information to a third party.

🔒 Browser analysis is performed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to the Digital Tattoo Flask server.

🧬 Browser Fingerprinting

Individual browser characteristics may seem harmless, but combinations of properties such as screen size, language, time zone, hardware information and browser configuration can contribute to a browser fingerprint. Digital Tattoo shows you which of these building blocks your browser reveals; it does not combine, hash or store them to identify or track you, and it makes no claim about any other website's behaviour. 🧮 Why this exposure level?

The exposure level is based on how many of the 8 browser-information categories tested by Digital Tattoo returned at least one value to this page without asking for permission. HIGH means 6–8 categories, MEDIUM means 4–5, and LOW means 0–3. This is a privacy-awareness classification, not a cybersecurity score. Permission-controlled resources are shown separately.

🛡️ How can I reduce browser exposure?

Different browsers expose different amounts of information. You can reduce unnecessary exposure without expecting every value to disappear.

  • Review and remove website permissions you no longer need.
  • Block third-party cookies or use your browser's stronger tracking protection.
  • Keep your browser updated so current privacy protections are available.
  • Use privacy-focused browser settings or anti-fingerprinting features where available.
  • Remember: “Not available” can simply mean this browser chose not to expose that API value.

🔒 Processed locally in your browser

Browser analysis is performed locally in your browser. Every value above was read by JavaScript running on this page and displayed directly on this page.

  • No browser data is sent to the Flask backend or any third party.
  • No fingerprint is stored in cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage.
  • No camera, microphone or GPS access is requested.
  • No external tracking or analytics scripts are loaded.
  • Closing or refreshing this page discards the whole report.

Note that Digital Tattoo cannot control what other websites you visit may collect, and a browser may still share some of this information with those sites.
