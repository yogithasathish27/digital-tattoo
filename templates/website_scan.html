🔍 Limitations — What This Scan Cannot See

  • Everything a website collects after interaction (forms, clicks, login).
  • Information collected after login or behind authentication.
  • Server-side data processing or database contents.
  • Complete JavaScript runtime behavior.
  • Every dynamically loaded tracker or resource.
  • Whether a website is completely safe or malicious.

This tool performs a passive analysis of publicly observable signals only. It is not a complete security audit and does not guarantee any level of website safety.

The Browser Privacy Scanner demonstrates information the browser can reveal to the current page — what your browser introduces before you say a word.

The Website Privacy Scanner examines publicly observable signals returned by a chosen public website — what the website exposes in its HTTP response and loaded resources.

The Website Scanner does not read Chrome/Firefox browsing history or inspect other open tabs. It only analyzes what the chosen website publicly returns when Digital Tattoo requests its homepage.
